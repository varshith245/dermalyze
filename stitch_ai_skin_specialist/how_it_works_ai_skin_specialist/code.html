Boundary & Transparency

Understanding the role of AI in your dermatological journey.

check_circle What this tool can do

  • check Identify common skin conditions (acne, eczema, dermatitis) with high accuracy.
  • check Flag urgent "Red Alert" signs that require immediate clinical attention.
  • check Provide over-the-counter care tips and general skin health guidance.
  • check Create a detailed summary you can share with your dermatologist.

cancel What this tool cannot do

  • close Provide a definitive medical diagnosis or replace a biopsy.
  • close Issue prescriptions for restricted medical treatments.
  • close Perform surgical procedures or physical skin exams.
  • close Replace the long-term relationship with your primary physician.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my skin data kept private? expand_more
Absolutely. All photos and voice recordings are encrypted and HIPAA-compliant. Your data is used solely for the analysis requested and is never sold to third parties.
How accurate is the AI compared to a doctor? expand_more
Our models achieve up to 94% accuracy for common conditions when high-quality photos are provided. However, AI is a screening tool, not a diagnostic one. It is meant to support, not replace, clinical judgment.
Does this work on all skin tones? expand_more
Yes. We have trained our vision models on diverse datasets representing the full Fitzpatrick skin type scale to ensure equitable and accurate analysis for everyone.
What if the AI flags something serious? expand_more
If the AI detects characteristics of high-risk conditions (like potential melanoma), it will issue an "Urgent Priority" flag and provide immediate instructions for seeking professional in-person care.
Is there a cost for the consultation? expand_more
We offer a basic initial screening for free. Comprehensive reports and multi-scan tracking are available through our premium clinical plan.
